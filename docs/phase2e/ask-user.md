# What we need from you to make these "same as Meowdoku"

Screenshots and short screen recordings from your phone are perfect. For each one, tell us whether it's from your **iPhone or Android**.

- **Please don't buy anything.**
- **Please don't uninstall Meowdoku on your main phone.** You could lose your progress.
- You can blur other players' names if you like. We only need the layout and the numbers.
- As before, we only measure from your material (numbers, timings, which screen comes when). We never copy its pictures or words, and your files stay out of our code.

## Leaderboard

1. **The ranking after a win.** Screenshot the ranking that pops up after you win a level. If it scrolls, take 2–3 shots so we see the whole list. Write down:
   - how many fish you had left when you won;
   - your ranking score just before and just after that win.

   If there's any other way to open the ranking (a button on the home screen, for example), screenshot that too.
2. **When the ranking ends.** When your ranking period is over (probably the next day), screenshot whatever appears: a result or reward screen, a "collect" button, a "watch a video to double" offer. Write down:
   - the clock time you saw it;
   - roughly when you first played the day before.

   If you ever finished 2nd or 3rd, what did you get?
3. **The hearts.** Tap the heart next to another player's name. Screenshot before and after, and tell us if anything happened.

## Ads

4. **First ads on a fresh install.** Only do this if you have a spare phone, or can borrow a family member's; don't use your main phone. Install Meowdoku fresh and play from level 1. Write down:
   - **the level where the first banner appeared at the bottom;**
   - **the level after which the first full-screen ad appeared;**
   - **the level where the Daily Challenge opened.** Screenshot it while it is still locked.

   If you can't do this, just tell us roughly when ads started for you, if you remember.
5. **Ten levels in a row.** Screen-record about 10 minutes of normal play, level after level, with one Retry. If recording is awkward, write down for each level instead:
   - the level number;
   - roughly how long it took;
   - whether a full-screen ad came after it;
   - whether the bottom banner was there **right at the start** of the level.
6. **Losing a level.** Make mistakes until you run out of fish, and record what happens:
   - Is a video offered to continue? If you watch it, how many fish do you get back?
   - Lose again in the same level. Is it offered a second time?
   - Say no to the video. Does a full-screen ad play? What about when you then tap Retry?
7. **A helper at zero.** Record tapping the cat helper when it shows the green video badge, then do the same with the bulb:
   - Does a question come first, or does the video start straight away?
   - How many do you get after the video?
   - **Is buying more ever offered there?**

   If you can, try once in airplane mode and screenshot what it says when no video is available.

## Purchases

8. **Shop or Premium screens.** Screenshot any shop, "Premium", "Premium Plus" or "No ads" screen you can find (Settings, or wherever it pops up). Show the full list of what each option includes and the prices exactly as shown. Tell us where you found it. On iPhone this is the Premium screen.

## Daily streak

9. **The streak.** Screenshot every place the game shows a streak or day counter: home, a pop-up when you open the app, or the daily screen. If you reach day 7, screenshot the reward (the "weekly reward"). Tell us:
   - what you did that day for it to count: played any level, played the daily, or just opened the app;
   - if you've ever missed a day, what happened: back to day 1, a "keep your streak" video, or nothing.

## Daily challenge

10. **One daily from start to finish.** Screenshot:
    - the daily on the home screen;
    - the daily screen, if there is one (is there a calendar or past days?);
    - the board at the start;
    - the result screen at the end.

    Write down your ranking score just before and just after winning the daily.
11. **A week of dailies and midnight.** For the next 7 days, take one screenshot of each day's daily at its start, so we can see the board size per weekday. Also, once, screenshot the daily just before and just after **midnight** your time. Tell us if the ranking or the streak changed at the same moment.

## Decisions (no phone needed)

12. **Four quick choices:**
    - **a) The banner during play on Facebook.** Facebook advises against it and stops all banner ads on **31 March 2027**. Keep it until then (as you asked), or leave it off?
    - **b) The leaderboard.** If Facebook has really removed worldwide leaderboards (we are checking), which do you want?
      - **(A)** your own daily ranking (your days, your best);
      - **(B)** rankings inside Facebook groups and chats;
      - **(C)** group tournaments;
      - **(D)** a real worldwide ranking on our own server (more work and running costs).

      We recommend A plus B, and D only if you're happy to run a server. We won't fake other players.
    - **c) Purchases in the Facebook iPhone app.** Turn them on once Facebook confirms they're allowed?
    - **d) What to sell during play, and at what price.** Today: 15 hints $1.99, 8 cat helpers $1.99, No Ads $3.99. Should we also sell mice? (In Meowdoku the mouse is always a video.)
