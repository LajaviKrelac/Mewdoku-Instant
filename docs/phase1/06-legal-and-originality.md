# 06 · Legal and originality: clean room, original assets, naming risk

Status: Phase 1 deliverable · Date: 2026-10-06

> **Not legal advice.** This is an engineering and process plan written by the game team. Before publishing anywhere public, especially the store listing name, get a review from a qualified IP lawyer in the relevant jurisdictions. Every legal assessment below is our own reasoning and is marked *inferred*.

## 1. Why this matters more than usual

- The original, Meowdoku by Oakever Games, is a top-chart hit with 10M+ installs on Google Play alone (01 §1.6).
- Its publisher is reported to be **in active IP disputes**. It lost one copyright case to Hungry Studio (Block Blast) and is fighting another brought by Avia Games, according to MobileGamer.biz on 2026-09-21 (01 §1.10; [mobilegamer]).
- The market is flooded with copycats (01 §2), and store reviewers and rights holders are alert to them.

Game **rules and mechanics** are generally treated as unprotectable ideas, and this puzzle type (Queens / 1-star Star Battle) predates Meowdoku. **Expression** is protectable: code, art, audio, text, specific level layouts and distinctive presentation. So is **branding**: names, logos and trade dress (*inferred*, general principle). Our plan copies only the rules and observable design facts, and expresses everything in our own way.

## 2. Clean-room process

| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1. Research (done, Phase 1) | We collected only **observable design facts** (rules, flow, controls, numbers, look described in words) into [01](01-game-deconstruction.md), with sources. We did not download, decompile or copy any of the original's code, art, audio or text. |
| 2. Specification (done, Phase 1) | Files [02](02-rebuild-spec.md) to [05](05-fbig-platform.md) turn those facts into **our own** spec. Wherever the original was unknown we made our own decision ([DECISION]). Timing values, colours and copy are freshly chosen and not taken from any teardown. |
| 3. Implementation (Phase 2) | Implementers work **from 02–05**. File 01 is background reading only. Nobody opens the sources in §4. |
| 4. Provenance | Phase 2 keeps `docs/provenance.md`. For every asset it records the author, the method (hand-coded SVG, synthesized sound, written copy, generated levels), the date and the tools used. |
| 5. Review | Every PR checklist includes: "No material from the original, its web build or the §4 sources; no tracing of screenshots; no copy-paste of store text." |
| 6. AI tools | If AI tools help with art or copy, prompts must **not** reference "Meowdoku", Oakever, or images or text of the original. Prompts and outputs are logged in the provenance file. |

The puzzle-engine prototype ([engine-prototype/](engine-prototype/)) was written by us from first principles. We read MIT-licensed open-source solvers ([samimsu], [cspuz], [grilops]) only to understand the algorithms; **no code was copied**. If any snippet is ever taken from an MIT project, its licence notice must be kept.

## 3. What we must not copy

| Category | Specific examples to avoid | Our alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Code** | The iOS/Android app (reportedly Godot), the Yandex web build's JS bundle, any decompiled or extracted source | Our own TypeScript (04) |
| **Art** | The tuxedo cat heads and their Spine animations, the trumpet-playing win cat, the crying cat hugging a broken heart, tile art, icons, app icon, store screenshots | A ginger "loaf" cat drawn as our own SVG, new poses, our own icon set (§5) |
| **Audio** | Any sound file, sound names (e.g. names from the bundle such as `mark_wrong_1`), jingles | WebAudio-synthesized sounds designed by us (02 §16) |
| **Text** | Store description phrases, including the rule names **"Exclusive Territory"** and **"The Aloof Rule"**, "guess right… guess wrong…" and "Non-Intrusive Ads…"; tutorial lines; hint sentences; praise-word list; rule-chip wording; UI strings | Our own copy (02 §9.1, §21) |
| **Values read from code** | Hex colours, ms timings, double-tap window, drag threshold, token counts and font stacks that the teardown tagged `[code]` | Freshly chosen values in `GameConfig` (02 §3) |
| **Level content** | The original's level layouts, which fan walkthrough sites and screenshot solvers reproduce (meowdoku.org, meowdokuonline.net, levelsolve.com, solver fixtures) | Levels generated by our engine (03) |
| **Third-party levels** | The samimsu community Queens levels (other people's content; 11 % are not unique) | Generated levels |
| **Branding** | The name "Meowdoku" or confusable names (§6), the logo, the sky-blue cat-ear caption band in store screenshots, the "Test your IQ" claim | Our own name, logo and listing style |
| **Trade dress combination** | The *combination* of tuxedo cat + cream page + orange pill buttons + white X on pastel tiles | Ginger cat, teal accent, dark X, region-aware gaps (02 §17) |

Functional elements that are allowed because they are generic to the genre or dictated by function (*inferred*):

- an N×N coloured grid;
- hearts as a mistake counter;
- a lightbulb hint and a reveal booster;
- rules shown above the board;
- a "Level N" title;
- tap to mark, double-tap to place;
- portrait layout.

We still vary the proportions and styling.

## 6. Naming and trademark risk: "Mewdoku" vs "Meowdoku"

### 6.1 Assessment (*inferred*; not legal advice)

| Factor | Observation |
|---|---|
| Visual similarity | "Mewdoku" vs "Meowdoku": one letter apart, with the same structure (cat sound + "-doku") |
| Phonetic similarity | "mew" and "meow" are both cat sounds and sound nearly the same in speech |
| Meaning | Both mean "cat-sound sudoku" |
| Goods and channel | Identical: casual puzzle games on mobile and web, the same puzzle concept, and cat pieces |
| Reputation of the senior mark | 10M+ installs, top-chart rankings and press coverage. Even unregistered, use in commerce can give rights (passing off or unfair competition) in some jurisdictions. |
| Registration status of "MEOWDOKU" | **Not searched**. Check USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO Global Brand Database, CNIPA, JPO and UKIPO. |
| Rights holder behaviour | Reported to be in active IP disputes (§1) |
| Platform review | Meta's review may reject names or listings that look confusingly similar to existing brands (*inferred*, check the policy) |
| Prior "Meowdoku" uses | A 2022 GitHub project, MiawDoku (2024) and many copycats (01 §2). None of this makes a similar name safe for us. |
| "-doku" suffix | "Sudoku" is generic in most markets; Nikoli holds "数独" as a mark in Japan (background knowledge, *inferred*). The suffix alone is low risk, but combined with "mew" it makes the name closer to "Meowdoku". |

**Bottom line (our read):** publishing as "Mewdoku" carries a **high** risk of a confusion claim, store rejection or takedown, especially given the publisher's litigation posture. It is fine as an **internal code name** (the repo name).

### 6.2 Options for the user (no decision made here)

These are brainstormed examples only. **None has been checked**, and some may already be in use.

| Option | Examples | Risk (our rough read) | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep "Mewdoku" | Mewdoku | High | Benefits from search spill-over, which is also exactly why it is risky |
| B. "Mewdoku" + subtitle | "Mewdoku: Cat Logic" | High | A subtitle does not fix similarity in the main name |
| C. Different "-doku" name, not close to "meow" | Pawdoku, Purrdoku, Nekodoku, Tabbydoku | Medium (clearance needed; "Catdoku" already exists as a copycat) | Keeps the sudoku-like discovery keyword |
| D. Distinct coined cat name, no "-doku" | Whisker Grid, Paws Apart, Catnap Logic, Kitty Corners, Lone Paws, Nine Lives Logic, Tabby Tiles | Low to medium (clearance needed) | Strongest differentiation; weaker search keyword (the listing description can still say "logic puzzle") |
| E. Descriptive name + studio brand | "Cat Placement Puzzle by <Studio>" | Low | Less memorable; hard to protect as our own brand |

Clearance steps for whichever option the user picks:

1. Search the trademark databases above in our target markets.
2. Search the App Store, Google Play, FB Instant Games, Yandex, Playgama and CrazyGames for identical or similar names.
3. Check domain and social handle availability.
4. Get a short lawyer review.
5. Only then design the logo and listing.

Also avoid using others' marks in our name or listing: "Meowdoku", "LinkedIn Queens", "Sudoku.com" and similar. A neutral genre description such as "a logic puzzle where each row, column and colour hides one cat" is fine (*inferred*).

## 7. Look-and-feel differentiation checklist (Phase 2 design review)

- [ ] Our cat is clearly not a tuxedo cat. Its silhouette and colours differ.
- [ ] The accent colour is not orange. Primary buttons use teal `--accent`.
- [ ] The X glyph is dark ink, not white.
- [ ] Region-aware gaps make our board read differently from evenly gapped tiles.
- [ ] Win and fail illustrations use our poses (party hat, bandage), not a trumpet cat or a crying cat with a broken heart.
- [ ] No sky-blue cat-ear caption band in store art; no "Test your IQ" claim.
- [ ] Rule chips use our own wording and icons.
- [ ] Every string comes from `i18n/en.ts` and was written by us.

## 8. Other legal items for Phase 4

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| Privacy policy | Required for the listing (*inferred*). It should cover: the FB player ID; game progress in FB player data and local storage; Meta ads (Audience Network), including any personalisation; `logEvent` analytics; no other data collection; a contact address. |
| Terms of use | Optional, but recommended |
| Age and audience | General audience, not aimed at children. Facebook accounts are 13+ (*inferred*). Avoid child-directed design or marketing. |
| Ad content | Ads come from the platform's network. Configure Monetization Manager category blocks if they are available (the original drew complaints about scam ads, 01 §11.11). |
| Claims | No unsupported cognitive or IQ claims in the listing or creatives |
| Third-party licences | Font (OFL), shown in About. No other runtime dependencies. Dev tooling licences do not ship. |
| Data portability | Not applicable in Phase 2 (no accounts beyond FB) |

## 9. Per-PR originality checklist (copy into the PR template in Phase 2)

- [ ] I did not open any source in 06 §4 while working on this change.
- [ ] Every new asset (SVG, sound recipe, text, level data) is ours and is listed in `docs/provenance.md`.

- [ ] Any third-party code or font comes with its licence notice.
