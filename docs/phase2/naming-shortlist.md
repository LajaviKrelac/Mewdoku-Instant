# Naming shortlist: replacing "Mewdoku"

Status: Phase 2 deliverable · Date: 2026-10-07 · Owner: product

> **Not legal advice.** This is a preliminary screen done by the game team. Before any name is used in public, get a proper clearance search and a review from a qualified IP lawyer (see [06 §6](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md)).

**Why we are renaming.** "Mewdoku" is one letter away from "Meowdoku" (Oakever Games), a 2026 chart-topping hit whose publisher is in active IP disputes. [06 §6](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md) rates the name as high risk. "Mewdoku" stays as the internal code name (the repo name) only.

**How the names were screened (2026-10-07).** We checked 41 candidates with web searches:

- **Stores and portals:** App Store, Google Play, Steam, itch.io, Poki, CrazyGames, Yandex, Playgama and Facebook.
- **Trademarks:** only the marks that showed up in search results. We did not query the USPTO, EUIPO or WIPO databases directly.
- **Language:** a pass over the 13 target languages.
- **Meowdoku:** a similarity rating for each name.

FB Instant Games titles can't be searched from the web, so "none found" there does not prove a name is free. Every conflict listed below is one the screen actually found. We added none.

**Ranking rule.** Names are sorted by risk first, then by appeal (1–5), then by how many caveats they carry and how serious those are. Any name already used by a game in the same genre was rejected.

**Recommendation.** Take **Loaf Logic** and **Paws Apart** into formal clearance, with **Cushy Cats** as the backup.

## 1. Shortlist (ranked)

| # | Name | Chars | Risk | Appeal | Like Meowdoku? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Loaf Logic** | 10 | Low | 4 | None | Lead candidate |
| 2 | **Paws Apart** | 10 | Low | 4 | None | Co-lead: the name states the no-touch rule |
| 3 | **Cushy Cats** | 10 | Low | 4 | None | Warm and safe, but needs a "logic puzzle" subtitle |
| 4 | **Tabby Turf** | 10 | Low (with caveats) | 4 | None | Strong logo, but the most caveats of the low-risk four |
| 5 | **Catnook** | 7 | Low | 4 | Low | Cosy, but must be tested against "Catdoku" |
| 6 | **Cat Plots** | 9 | Low | 3 | None | Pun on our colour regions, but noisy search results |

### 1. Loaf Logic

- **Why it works.**
  - It names our own mascot, the ginger loaf cat.
  - It alliterates, it is easy to say, and "Logic" tells players what kind of game this is.
  - "To loaf" means to laze, which suits the calm tone.
  - In British English, "use your loaf" means "use your head".
- **Conflicts.**
  - No game, app or mark called "Loaf Logic" was found.
  - Neighbours we did find:
    - Lodge Logic cast-iron loaf pans (cookware).
    - Cat-loaf puzzles with different names: Loaf Cat Sliding and LoafyCat, both on iOS.
    - Same-genre "cat + logic" titles: CatLogic, Cat Logic Puzzle and Cats Logic. Because of these, "Logic" is generic and "Loaf" has to carry the brand.
    - Loaf, a UK furniture retailer selling different goods. The check noted this from general knowledge, not from a search result.
- **Linguistic.**
  - No awkward meaning in any target language.
  - The "cat loaf" meme is mostly known on the English-speaking internet. Many players will read "loaf" as bread.
  - Spanish, Portuguese and Italian readers may say "lo-af". The mascot art teaches the meme.
- **Wordmark.** The ginger loaf cat takes the place of the "o" in "Loaf". "Logic" is set in teal rounded type, and its "o" is a small pastel tile.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A full USPTO/EUIPO/WIPO knockout search in classes 9, 41 and 28.
  - A search of the FB Instant Games directory, which has to be done in the app.
  - A check of how far existing "LOAF" marks reach.
  - Domain and social handles.

### 2. Paws Apart

- **Why it works.**
  - The name states our key rule: cats must not touch.
  - It is a pun on "worlds apart".
  - Two short words make a clean logo.
  - It was the best-cleared candidate in the screen.
- **Conflicts.**
  - No game, app or "PAWS APART" mark was found.
  - Unrelated uses: "A Paw Apart Pet Sitting Services" (a Facebook page for a small business), a "paws" theme at Apart jewellery, and a figurine.
  - The word PAWS on its own has registrations, for example Paws With A Cause (serial 74184793), so the lawyer should clear the full two-word name, not just the single word.
  - Many games start with "Paws …", but none is called Paws Apart.
- **Linguistic.**
  - "Paws" sounds like "pause", so some players will search for "Pause Apart".
  - Brazilian Portuguese readers may hear "paus" (sticks, but also vulgar slang). This is a mild risk.
  - Neutral or positive meanings elsewhere: Indonesian "paus" means whale, German "apart" means chic, and French "à part" means separate.
- **Wordmark.** "Paws" and "Apart" each sit on their own pastel tile with a clear teal gap between them, and a ginger paw print on each tile.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A knockout search on the two-word name in classes 9 and 41.
  - The FB Instant Games directory.
  - A quick check with Brazilian players on the "paus" reading.
  - Nobody searches for "paws apart", so the store description must carry the search terms, such as "cat logic puzzle".

### 3. Cushy Cats

- **Why it works.** It is warm, soft, alliterative and plainly about cats, which suits a calm, all-ages Facebook audience.
- **Conflicts.**
  - No game, app or mark was found.
  - Nearby, in different goods:
    - "Cushy", a cat-toy line from duvo+ and Just Russel.
    - Barker Meow's "Cushy Cat Bed".
    - Articles that use the phrase descriptively.
  - Because the phrase is descriptive, it is harder to own, and searches for it bring up cat beds and toys.
- **Linguistic.**
  - "Cushy" is not widely known outside English, but it sounds friendly.
  - It comes from Hindi/Urdu "khush" (happy), which is a nice fit for Hindi speakers.
  - In Portuguese it starts with "cu" (vulgar). This is minor, because the word is pronounced "cushy".
  - There is a faint echo of "kush" (cannabis slang) in English. Low risk.
- **Wordmark.** The ginger loaf cat on a pastel, cushion-shaped tile under rounded teal type, with both Cs drawn as matching soft curves.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A knockout search in classes 9 and 41. Add class 28 if we plan plush or other merch, since that is the class the Cushy toy line falls in.
  - The FB Instant Games directory.
  - Test a subtitle such as "Cushy Cats: Cat Logic Puzzle", because the name alone gives no hint of logic.

### 4. Tabby Turf

- **Why it works.**
  - "Cat + territory" fits the "one cat per colour region" rule.
  - The alliteration makes a strong logo.
  - Our ginger mascot is an orange tabby.
- **Conflicts.** None for the exact name. The closest finds were:
  - "Kitty Turf", a small prototype cat RTS on itch.io.
  - TABBY application 98237991 (filed 2023, with a cat-head logo) for "toys, games and playthings; video game apparatus". That is class 28, not the software classes 9 and 41, but it still needs a lawyer's read.
  - A registered TABBY mark for a dating app for cat people.
  - "Turf", a GPS territory game.
  - Tabby, a large buy-now-pay-later fintech in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider Gulf.
  - From the other screens: Tabbydoku (iOS, July 2026) already uses "tabby" as the brand word for our exact rules.
- **Linguistic.**
  - Outside English, few people know "tabby" as a cat word.
  - In Arabic-speaking markets it calls up the fintech brand.
  - French "le turf" means horse racing and betting, a mild gambling link.
  - Dutch "turf" and Russian "торф" mean peat.
  - English "turf war" sounds combative for a calm game.
- **Wordmark.** "Tabby Turf" in teal with ginger tabby stripes across both Ts, and the loaf cat sitting on a single pastel region tile.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A lawyer's read on TABBY 98237991 and on the TABBY dating-app registration.
  - A search for Tabby fintech marks in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.
  - A check of how much store search overlaps with Tabbydoku.
  - A check with French and Arabic speakers.

### 5. Catnook

- **Why it works.** It says "cat" clearly, a nook is a cosy corner, it makes a compact one-word wordmark, and it suits the calm tone.
- **Conflicts.**
  - No game or software uses the name.
  - Local cat businesses do:
    - Catnook Cattery in Geelong, Australia, which has the facebook.com/catnook.com.au page.
    - Cat Nook Cafe in Norwood, PA.
  - No CATNOOK mark was found. The closest is KATNOOK ESTATE (wine).
  - Associations: Barnes & Noble's NOOK (a large class 9 portfolio) and Tom Nook from Animal Crossing.
- **Linguistic.**
  - Non-native speakers often don't know "nook". Some will read it as "nuke" or say "cat-NO-ok".
  - No offensive meanings.
- **Against the brief.** "Catnook" and "Catdoku" are both 7 letters, both start with "Cat", and have a similar shape (C-A-T-?-O-?-K). The brief rules out names that could be confused with Catdoku, so this must be tested. The two-word variant "Cat Nooks" (also screened: low risk, appeal 3) weakens the shape match.
- **Wordmark.** The loaf cat tucked into the corner notch of a pastel tile, with "Catnook" in teal rounded type.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A confusion test against "Catdoku".
  - A knockout search in classes 9 and 41 that includes NOOK.
  - A Facebook handle plan, such as "catnookgame", because "catnook" is effectively taken.
  - The FB Instant Games directory.

### 6. Cat Plots

- **Why it works.**
  - It starts with "cat", a word players search for.
  - It has a double meaning: garden plots (our colour regions) and cats plotting (playful scheming).
- **Conflicts.**
  - No game, app or mark was found.
  - Search noise from data-science "catplot": seaborn's `catplot`, the CatPlot Python library and CatPlot astronomy software.
  - Sound-alikes: the "Cat-a-Pult" toy kit and an App Store stacking game.
- **Linguistic.**
  - Harmless meanings elsewhere:
    - Indonesian and Malay "cat" means paint.
    - Czech, Slovak and Polish "plot/płot" means fence.
    - Russian "плот" means raft.
    - French "plot" means a block.
  - Said quickly, it can sound like "catapults".
- **Wordmark.** Pastel patches laid out like garden plots, each with one tiny ginger loaf, and "Cat Plots" in teal above them.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - A knockout search in classes 9 and 41.
  - The FB Instant Games in-app catalogue.
  - A check of how we would rank in store search against the "catplot" noise.
  - Choose this over "Paw Plots", which has the Brazilian "pau" problem.

### 7. Loafdoku

- **Why it works.**
  - It is the cleanest "-doku" option. It keeps the sudoku-style search keyword ([06 §6.2](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md) option C) without using a cat sound.
  - It shares the "Loaf" root with #1, so the two could share a wordmark.
- **Conflicts.**
  - No exact match was found.
  - Nearby cat-loaf puzzles with different names: Loafuzzle (an HTML5 block puzzle on itch.io), Loaf Cat Sliding (iOS) and Catloaf (itch.io).
  - The "-doku" field is crowded: Catdoku, Tabbydoku, Pawdoku and Panda Doku.
  - The search index barely covers FB Instant Games, Poki, CrazyGames, Yandex and Playgama.
- **Linguistic.**
  - Most non-English players will read "loaf" as bread.
  - "-doku" has harmless meanings elsewhere, shared by every "-doku" name:
    - Turkish: tissue.
    - Indonesian slang: money.
    - German: documentary.
    - Japanese 毒, out of context: poison.
- **Caution.** A "<word> + doku" name still sits among the cat "-doku" copycats that the rename is meant to escape. Its similarity to Meowdoku is rated low, not none.
- **Wordmark.** The same idea as Loaf Logic: the loaf cat as the "o" in "Loaf", with "doku" in lighter teal on a short strip of pastel tiles.
- **Remaining clearance.**
  - Direct searches on FB Instant Games, Poki, CrazyGames, Yandex and Playgama.
  - A knockout search in classes 9 and 41.
  - Domain and social handles.
  - A lawyer's view on whether any "-doku" name keeps us too close to Meowdoku.

## 2. Rejected and why

### A. A cat game or a same-genre game already uses the name or a near-identical one (high risk)

| Name | Main reason |
|---|---|
| Pawdoku | At least 3 cat Queens-style games already use it: Google Play [`com.pawdoku.puzzle.game`](https://apkcombo.com/pawdoku/com.pawdoku.puzzle.game/), App Store id6782894084, and brainbuff.club. It also rhymes with Meowdoku |
| Purrdoku | 2 same-genre games on Google Play (`com.ols.purrdoku`, `com.purrdoku.game`). Cat sound + "-doku" is the Meowdoku formula |
| Nekodoku | 2 same-genre games on Google Play, one with the package id `com.miustudio.nekodoku.meowdoku`. ネコドク can read as "cat poison" |
| Tabbydoku | "Tabbydoku - Cat Sudoku" on iOS (July 2026) has identical rules. It was listed in 06 §6.2 option C and is now taken |
| Kittydoku | "KittyDoku: Cat Sudoku Puzzle" is live on iOS (id6776476910) with identical rules, and the name follows the Meowdoku formula |
| Kitty Corners | Freeway Games released a game with identical rules under this name in July 2026, on Steam, mobile and [Playgama](https://playgama.com/game/kitty-corners) |
| Pawzzle | PlaySimple released the Queens-style "Pawzzle!" on iOS on 30 Jul 2026. Other apps and PAWZZLE filings also exist |
| Purrimeter | A live web game with exactly our rules uses it (a small hobby project) |
| Catnap Logic | "[Catnap](https://catnap.outwit.games/)" is a daily Queens browser game that calls itself "Meowdoku-style". CatNap is also the horror villain from Poppy Playtime |
| Logicat | "Logicat: Brain Puzzle" is on Google Play and "Logicats" is on Steam. "CatLogic", with the same rules as ours, is on Android |
| Purrcels | Three cat games are called "Purrcel": an itch.io puzzle-adventure, an Android demo (Oct 2026) and a jam entry. Hard to spell, and reads as "piglet" in Italian and Catalan |

### B. Crowded names, or names that nearly collide with others (medium risk)

| Name | Main reason |
|---|---|
| Whiskerdoku | The highest Meowdoku-confusion risk in the set: cat word + "-doku", the same rules, and 7+ cat "-doku" clones |
| Purr Logic | One letter from "Pure Logic" (a logic puzzle on Steam and Switch) and "Purry Logic" (a cat book). CatLogic's tagline is "The Purr-fect Logic Puzzle Game". It repeats the one-letter problem we are escaping |
| Purr Puzzle | "Purr in Bloom" (Steam, Aug 2026) has exactly our rules. Purrfect Puzzle and Purrzle are close. The name is descriptive |
| Purrfect Spot | "Purrfect" is crowded (Purrfect Difference, Purrfect Puzzle, Purrfect Home and others), and "Spot" suggests spot-the-difference |
| Tabby Tiles | Tabbydoku already pairs "tabby" with our rules. Meow Tiles (Oink Games) has the same shape. "Tiles" suggests Mahjong or tile-matching |
| Calico Grid | "Calico" already names an award-winning puzzle about cats and colour patches (board game and digital version) and a cat-café sim |
| Seat the Cats | "Catty Seats", a cat placement-logic puzzle, is live on the App Store |
| Whisker Room | A cat café already uses "The Whisker Room" and holds @whiskerroom on Facebook. WHISKER is a registered mark of the Litter-Robot maker |
| Whiskerwise | Several pet brands use the exact name (one claims TM), and there is a pet puzzle toy called Whisker Wise |
| Nap Map | "NapMap" is a baby-sleep app on iOS. The name echoes Snapchat's "Snap Map" and gives no hint of cats or logic |
| Sunpatch | "Sleepy Sun Patch" is a cat web game, and cats in sunny patches are a common cosy-game theme. Gamers read "patch" as a software update |
| Paw Plots | Clean legally, but has the Brazilian "pau" reading. "Cat Plots" (#6) is the better form |
| Nine Lives Logic | 16 characters, over the limit. "Nine Lives" is already a cat puzzle on Steam and the title of many other games. In Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German and Arabic, cats have seven lives |

### C. Low risk but weak (keep as reserve names, mode names or taglines)

| Name | Main reason |
|---|---|
| Whisker Grid | Clean on the exact name, but "Whisker" is a crowded prefix. WHISKER is a registered mark of the Litter-Robot maker, which also runs a cat app. Searches for "Whisker grid" return Whisker Labs power-grid news. Best fallback |
| Cat Nooks | Variant of Catnook (#5) |
| Neko Grid | Brand-safety problem for 13+: searches for "neko" bring up anime catgirl content, some of it adult, and the word has a slang meaning. "Neko" is also a crowded prefix |
| Paw Squares | Flat. "Paw" reads as dog (PAW Patrol), and it has the Brazilian "pau" reading |
| Ginger Grid | Outside English, "ginger" just means the spice. gingergrid.com is taken. Close to Outfit7's Talking Ginger |
| Lone Paws | Sounds sad, like an animal-rescue charity, and has no puzzle keyword. Possible mode name |
| Napdoku | Barely hints at cats. Its shape (three letters + "doku") echoes Mewdoku and Meowdoku, and it is one letter from "Nodoku" |
| Loafspace | Reads as a bakery, a furniture shop or a sci-fi game, and may be misread as "Loftspace" |
| Cat Quarters | Flat and institutional. The best-known existing use is a litter-box enclosure |
| One Cat Each | A rule, not a brand, and searches for it return Meowdoku-style store copy. Possible tagline ("Loaf Logic: one cat each") |

"Nine Lives" could also become a mode name, for example a challenge mode that allows nine mistakes.

## 3. Next steps for clearance (before Phase 4)

These steps are not legal advice. Run them on the 2–3 finalists before anyone designs the logo or writes the store listing.

1. **Pick the finalists.** The owner picks 2–3 names from §1. We suggest Loaf Logic, Paws Apart and Cushy Cats.
2. **Trademark knockout search.** Search exact spellings, sound-alikes and close variants in class 9 (game software) and class 41 (online games). Add class 28 if we plan plush or other merch.
   - USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, and the WIPO Global Brand Database, which covers Madrid filings.
   - National offices in priority markets, such as INPI (Brazil), CNIPA, JPO, KIPO, IP India, DGIP (Indonesia), TÜRKPATENT and Rospatent. Add the UAE and Saudi Arabia if Gulf players matter (this is required for Tabby Turf).
   - Also check whether MEOWDOKU is registered. [06 §6.1](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md) lists that as not yet searched.
3. **Store and portal search.**
   - Where to look:
     - The FB Instant Games in-app catalogue, since its titles can't be searched from the web.
     - Messenger.
     - The App Store and Google Play in several countries.
     - Steam and itch.io.
     - Poki, CrazyGames, Yandex Games and Playgama.
   - What to look for: identical names, and same-genre cat games with similar names.
4. **Domains and handles.**
   - Domains: the .com, plus a fallback such as .game, .games or "<name>game.com".
   - Social: the Facebook Page name and vanity URL, Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube.
   - Once a name is chosen, reserve matching handles everywhere.
5. **Native-speaker check.** A short check in each of the 13 target languages, focused on the points flagged above:
   - Brazilian Portuguese: "paw"/"paus" and the "cu-" start.
   - Arabic: Tabby.
   - French: turf.
   - Japanese: how the name looks in katakana.
6. **Platform policy.** Check Meta's FB Instant Games naming and review rules on similarity to existing brands. This point is *inferred* in [06 §6.1](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md).
7. **Lawyer review.** Get a short clearance opinion on the chosen name in our main markets, and advice on whether and where to file before launch.
8. **Then:**
   - Update 06 §6.2: Tabbydoku, Pawdoku, Purrdoku, Nekodoku, Kitty Corners and Catnap are now known to be taken.
   - Design the wordmark.
   - Write the store listing using neutral genre wording ("a logic puzzle where each row, column and colour hides one cat").
   - Keep "Mewdoku" as the internal code name only.
