# Naming: "-doku" options and the Pandoku question

Status: Phase 2 deliverable · Date: 2026-10-08 · Owner: product · Follows [naming-shortlist.md](naming-shortlist.md)

> **Not legal advice.** This is a preliminary screen done by the game team. Before any name is used in public, get a proper clearance search and a review from a qualified IP lawyer (see [06 §6](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md)).

**Short answer.**

- **Pandoku is taken.** At least 3 live games use the exact name with our exact rules (iOS, Google Play, Playgama), and every other panda + "doku" form is taken too. Reject it.
- **Don't switch to pandas.** Pandas are the second most-cloned animal in this genre, and a black-and-white panda brings back the look of the original's tuxedo cat.
- **Keep the ginger loaf cat.** If the name must end in "-doku", take **Loafdoku** (lead) and **Yarndoku** (backup) into clearance, with **Nookdoku** in reserve.
- If the lawyer says any "-doku" name keeps us too close to Meowdoku, fall back to **Loaf Logic** from the earlier shortlist.

**How the names were screened (2026-10-08).**

- Web searches across the App Store, Google Play, itch.io, web portals (Playgama, Poki, CrazyGames, Yandex) and GitHub, plus a pass over the 13 target languages and a Meowdoku similarity rating.
- Trademarks: only the marks that showed up in search results. No registry (USPTO, EUIPO, WIPO, JPO) was queried directly.
- FB Instant Games titles can't be searched from the web, so "none found" there proves nothing.
- Playgama, appgoblin and BlueStacks pages could not be opened (DNS blocked), so their details come from search snippets.
- The shared search budget ran out partway through. Names that got only one search are flagged.
- Every conflict listed here is one the checks actually found.

**Ranking rule** (same as the shortlist). Names are sorted by risk first, then by appeal (1–5), then by how many caveats they carry and how serious those are. Any name already used by a game in the same genre is rejected.

## 1. Pandoku: verdict

**Not usable. Reject.** It is the best-sounding "-doku" name (appeal 4–5), and its similarity to Meowdoku is low. But it is the most collided name screened in either round.

Every "-doku" name shares these harmless readings: Turkish "doku" = tissue, German "Doku" = documentary, Indonesian slang "doku" = money, and Japanese ドク = 毒 (poison) when read out of context. They are not repeated below.

### Keep the cat

**C1. Loafdoku**

- **Why:**
  - It names our own mascot and keeps all the current art.
  - It shares the "Loaf" root and the wordmark with Loaf Logic, the earlier lead.
  - It has no cat sound in it.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name. Three queries were run, including Loaf-doku, Loaf doku and Loafydoku.
  - Neighbours: [Loafuzzle](https://leopardlotus.itch.io/loafuzzle) (cat-loaf block puzzle, itch.io) and [Lootdoku](https://github.com/danielsarabuna/lootdoku) (Unity block puzzle).
  - Searching "Loafdoku cat loaf logic puzzle" returns only the cat "-doku" crowd, Meowdoku included. Store search will put us next to it.
- **Language:**
  - Outside English, most players read "loaf" as bread.
  - Spanish, Portuguese and Italian speakers may say "lo-af".
  - The "f-d" cluster is slightly awkward.
- **Logo:** the loaf cat forms the "o" in "Loaf", with "doku" in lighter teal on a strip of pastel tiles.

**C2. Yarndoku**

- **Why:**
  - The ball of yarn reads as "cat" in every culture, even where the word "yarn" is unknown.
  - It has no Meowdoku echo at all.
  - In Turkish, "doku" also means weave, which fits.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name.
  - Neighbours:
    - [WoolDoku](https://dev.bukkit.org/server-mods/wooldoku/), a Minecraft sudoku plugin.
    - [Yendoku](https://budjetti.itch.io/yendoku-a-sudoku-roguelike), a sudoku roguelike two letters away.
    - Heavy store noise from the yarn-sort genre (Yarn Puzzle, Yarn Fever!, Poki's World of Yarn), so players may expect a sorting game.
  - A registered [YARN mark](https://trademark.justia.com/863/38/yarn-86338823.html) exists (reg. 4928251, media/metadata software and social networking; owner not shown in results).
  - Meta originated the Yarn package manager, which matters because we ship on Facebook.
- **Language:**
  - No negative meanings found.
  - Turkish "yarın" means tomorrow, which is harmless.
  - Some Spanish speakers may say "JARN".
- **Logo:** a ginger yarn thread traces the region borders under the word, and the loaf cat bats the ball that forms the "o".

**C3. Nookdoku**

- **Why:** a cosy corner suits the calm tone and "one cat per region". It is fun to say.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name.
  - Neighbours:
    - [Knotdoku](https://lcbgamestudio.itch.io/knotdoku), an itch.io sudoku prototype.
    - [Nodoku](https://gamesrightmeow.itch.io/nodoku), on itch.io.
    - Noku Puzzle, on iOS.
  - Associations rather than conflicts: Barnes & Noble's NOOK (a large class 9 portfolio) and Tom Nook (Nintendo).
- **Language:**
  - "Nook" is little known outside English. Players may read it as "nuke", and the "oo" invites typos.
  - No negative meanings found.
- **Logo:** the double "oo" drawn as two rounded tile corners, with the loaf cat asleep in one.

**C4. Gingerdoku**

- **Why:** it names the mascot's colour, keeps all the art, and is easy to spell.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name.
  - Main concern: Outfit7's [Talking Ginger](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.outfit7.talkinggingerfree), a ginger kitten from a large brand that enforces its marks. That is the same animal and the same colour as ours.
  - gingergrid.com is taken (earlier screen).
- **Language:**
  - Outside English, "ginger" reads as the spice, so the cat-colour meaning is lost.
  - In the UK it is mild teasing for redheads.
  - At 10 letters it is long for an icon label.
- **Logo:** tabby stripes across the "G", with the loaf cat on the strip of tiles. Avoid any Talking Ginger look.

Held back from the ranking: **Fluffdoku** and **Snugdoku**. Both are low risk with appeal 3, but each got only one search (see §4).

### Switch animal

The re-theme estimate in §3 was made for pandas. The same art, copy and test files would change for any new animal; that part is our inference, not a separate estimate.

**A1. Polardoku** (polar bear)

- **Why:** the cleanest exact-name screen of all the non-cat names. No polar-bear game with our rules was found.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name. The only hits were PolarDread (VR horror) and Polaroid.
  - Neighbours:
    - Nintendo's Polarium (a DS puzzle game).
    - [Thin Ice](https://meepmoop.itch.io/thin-ice), a penguin Star Battle game on itch.io.
  - Marks: [POLAR](https://trademarks.justia.com/856/97/polar-85697505.html) (Polar Electro, reg. 1836604, class 9, heart-rate-monitor software) and other POLAR-formative class 9 filings. These cover different goods, but a lawyer should read them.
- **Language:**
  - "Polar" doesn't mean "bear" on its own and can read as maths or physics.
  - Turkish "polar" means fleece.
  - From general knowledge, not a search: in Latin America it suggests Cerveza Polar (beer).
- **Logo:** a round polar-bear head peeking over a pale ice-blue tile, with "doku" in teal. This needs a full cold-palette art change.

**A2. Octodoku** (octopus)

- **Why:**
  - No Queens-style octopus game was found.
  - A colour-changing octopus fits the colour regions.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name.
  - The main neighbour is a logic puzzle: [OCTO Logic Puzzle](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/octo-logic-puzzle/id1508794281) (iOS), marked "OCTO®" on its site.
  - Marks:
    - OCTOPUZZ application (serial 98873695).
    - OCTO MEMO (reg. 6382691, children's educational games).
  - The lawyer should check OCTO in classes 9, 28 and 41.
- **Language:**
  - "Octo" (eight) is read correctly everywhere, which promises 8×8 boards only. Ours run from 5×5 to 12×12.
  - Italian "la piovra" is a mafia metaphor, and Japanese タコ can be a mild insult. Both are minor with cute round art.
  - Less cuddly than a cat for a calm Facebook audience.
- **Logo:** a small round octopus whose eight arms each hold a different pastel tile.

**A3. Hedgedoku** (hedgehog)

- **Why:**
  - The exact-name screen is clean.
  - The "hedgehog's dilemma" story suits the no-touch rule.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name. Also checked: Hedgehogdoku, Hogdoku and Pricklydoku.
  - [Prickle: Hedgehog Logic Puzzle](https://psprices.com/region-sg/game/20249643/prickle-hedgehog-logic-puzzle) has exactly our rules. It has a different name, but it overlaps with us in store search.
  - Spelling neighbours: HedgeDoc (a markdown editor) and Hexdoku (Steam).
- **Language:**
  - "Hedge" is English-only. The local words are erizo, Igel, kirpi, ёж, ハリネズミ and so on.
  - Where "hedge" is known, it suggests finance.
  - The "-dged-" cluster is hard to spell, and French speakers drop the "h".
- **Logo:** a curled hedgehog forms the "o" in "Hedge", with its quills drawn as short strokes. It must not look like Sonic.

**A4. Bamboodoku** (giant panda)

- **Why:** the only panda-themed name whose exact form is clear. "Bamboo" is understood almost everywhere.
- **Conflicts:**
  - None found for the exact name. Also checked: Bambudoku, Bambooku and Bamboku.
  - Neighbours:
    - [Судоку Бамбук](https://yandex.ru/games/app/500253) ("Sudoku Bamboo"), a number sudoku on Yandex Games.
    - Physical "Bamboo Sudoku" board sets.
    - BambooKu, an Indonesian bedding brand.
  - [Pandadoku Logic](https://urnpaula.com/) already markets "bamboo-themed environments".
  - Everyday Grids has a PANDA mode with bamboo (re-theme check).
  - Store search would place it among 10 or more same-rule panda clones.
  - BAMBOO marks in classes 9 and 41 (for example Wacom's Bamboo tablets) need clearing.
- **Language:**
  - Portuguese "bambo" means slack, which is harmless.
  - At 10 letters and 4 syllables it is long, and the "oo-do" sound repeats.
- **Logo:** the "oo" in "Bamboo" drawn as two bamboo-stalk rings, with a panda peeking between them.
- **Verdict:** consider it only if the owner insists on pandas after reading §3.

## 3. Switching to pandas: what it takes

**Effort.** About 4–6 person-days of build work, or about 2–3 days with parallel workstreams. The code is well abstracted: the board, HUD and overlays only reference sprite IDs and i18n keys, and the audio has no cat sounds. On top of the build comes a full redo of the name screen and clearance (1–2 days of work plus calendar time).

| Area | Files | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Board head (ears, patches, 7 eye states, 5 mouths, blink). The "sad" mood needs a new cue, because round ears can't droop. It must read at about 18 px on 12×12 boards | `src/ui/art/cat-parts.ts` | L (1–1.5 d) |
| Mascot body and poses | `src/ui/art/mascot.ts`, `src/ui/art/illustrations.ts` | M (1 d) |
| Sprite symbols. Keep the `#cat-*` IDs; redraw `icon-paw` | `src/ui/art/sprite.ts` | S–M (2–4 h) |
| Palette tokens and contrast checks. The ink outline vanishes on black fur | `palette.ts`, `src/styles/tokens.css`, `scripts/palette-check.ts` | S (2–3 h) |
| Booster colour, background dots, favicon, title and meta | `hud.css`, `base.css`, `public/favicon.svg`, `index.html` | S (1–2 h) |
| Copy: 48 of 228 strings. About 30 are mechanical swaps and about 18 need rewriting (puns, tagline, alt text, booster). Keys stay | `src/i18n/en.ts` | M (0.5–1 d) |
| Tests: about 20 hard-coded strings in 9 files | `tests/unit/**` | S (1–2 h) |
| Docs and screenshots (02 §17.3, 06 §3/§5/§7, provenance, differences-vs-original) | `docs/**` | M (0.5–1 d) |
| QA: palette check and small-board legibility | none | about 0.5 d |
| Audio, UI logic | `src/audio/sfx.ts`, `src/ui/**` | None |

**Keep every internal identifier** (`kitty`, `cat`, `mewdoku.save.v1`, the `mewdoku-gen/` tag, seeds). Renaming them orphans saves or regenerates all 1,000 levels and 823 dailies, and it breaks `verify-levels`.

**Risks.**

1. **Pandas are already taken in this genre.** Pandoku (Playgama, iOS) and Panda-ku! have our rules and already use the "pandas can't touch" pitch.
2. **It moves us closer to the original, not further.** The original's piece is a tuxedo cat: black fur, white face. 02 §17.3 chose ginger precisely to avoid black-and-white, and 06 §7 requires both silhouette and colours to differ. A panda head on pastel tiles brings that colour scheme back.
3. **Brand neighbours:** Panda Pop, Kung Fu Panda, BabyBus "Baby Panda", the WWF panda logo, and PandaDoc next to any "Panda-doc/-doku" name.
4. **It reads as a kids' app.** That conflicts with 06 §8's 13+ general audience. This is inferred and needs a check.
5. **Readability at 18 px.** Moods sit in eyes inside black patches, and the dark fur sits next to the dark-ink X marks.
6. **Weaker puns, and sunk work.** The 41-name cat screen, the provenance rows and the screenshots would need redoing. Phase 2b art (task #37) should wait until the theme is settled.

**Recommendation: don't switch.** The build cost is modest, but a panda buys less distinctiveness, not more.

- Keep the ginger loaf cat. It is already clearly unlike the original's tuxedo cat.
- Take **Loafdoku** and **Yarndoku** into clearance, with **Loaf Logic** as the non-"-doku" fallback.
- Under any theme, rename the visible "Kitty" booster label to "Paw". It is the same name the original uses for its booster ([differences-vs-original §2.5](differences-vs-original.md)). The change is about 10 `en.ts` values and 2 test strings.
- If the team still wants a panda feel, a red panda is the only variant that avoids black-and-white and reuses the ginger tokens. It would need its own name screen, because Tandoku is rejected (§4).

## 5. Next clearance steps

These steps are not legal advice. Run them on the chosen finalists (we suggest Loafdoku and Yarndoku, with Loaf Logic as the fallback) before anyone designs a logo or writes the store listing.

1. **Trademark knockout search.** Search exact spellings, sound-alikes and close variants in class 9 (game software) and class 41 (online games). Add class 28 if we plan plush or other merch.
   - Registries: USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO and the WIPO Global Brand Database, plus INPI (Brazil), CNIPA, KIPRIS (Korea), IP India, DGIP (Indonesia), TÜRKPATENT and Rospatent.
   - **JPO (J-PlatPat).** "Sudoku" is a Nikoli trademark in Japan. Ask whether a "-doku" coinage, or ドク in katakana, could be seen as close to it, and search for "-doku" marks already on the register.
   - Marks named in this screen: LOAF; YARN (reg. 4928251); NOOK; POLAR (reg. 1836604); OCTO, OCTOPUZZ and OCTO MEMO; BAMBOO; Outfit7's Talking Ginger.
   - Check whether MEOWDOKU is registered. [06 §6.1](../phase1/06-legal-and-originality.md) lists it as not yet searched.
2. **Store and portal search.**
   - Where to look:
     - The FB Instant Games in-app catalogue and Messenger.
     - The App Store and Google Play in several countries.
     - itch.io and Steam.
     - Playgama (which distributes to other portals), Poki, CrazyGames and Yandex Games.
   - What to look for: the exact name, one- and two-letter variants (Loafydoku; Yendoku and WoolDoku; Knotdoku and Nodoku), and same-genre cat games.
3. **Re-open the pages the screen couldn't fetch** (Playgama, appgoblin, BlueStacks), but only if anyone needs to cite the Pandoku findings outside the team. The verdict doesn't depend on them.
4. **Domains and handles.** These were not checked in this screen.
   - Domains: the .com, plus a fallback such as .game, .games or "<name>game.com".
   - Social: the Facebook Page name and vanity URL, Instagram, TikTok, X and YouTube.
5. **Native-speaker check.**
   - Japanese: the katakana form and the 毒 ("poison") reading.
   - Turkish: "doku" (tissue).
   - German: "Doku" (documentary).
   - Spanish, Portuguese and Italian: how players say "loaf" and "yarn".
6. **Platform policy.** Check Meta's FB Instant Games naming and review rules on similarity to existing titles.
7. **Lawyer review.** Get a short clearance opinion on the chosen name in our main markets. In particular, ask whether any "<word> + doku" name keeps enough distance from Meowdoku, given the 2026 animal-doku clone field, and whether and where to file before launch.
8. **Then:**
   - Update 06 §6.2 and naming-shortlist.md with the names now known to be taken: Pandoku, Panda Doku, Pandadoku, Koaladoku, Axodoku, Owldoku, Foxydoku, Hamdoku, Cubedoku and Catdoku.
   - Rename the visible "Kitty" booster label.
   - Keep "Mewdoku" as the internal code name only.
